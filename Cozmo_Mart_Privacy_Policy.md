# Cozmo Mart Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 8, 2026**

Cozmo Mart is an e-commerce application for browsing and purchasing Cozmo products, vitamins, and related products. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how users can contact us or request deletion of their account and personal information.

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Cozmo Mart may collect:

- **Account information:** name, email address, phone number, and authentication information when you create or use an account.
- **Social login information:** if you sign in with Google or Apple, we may receive information made available by the selected sign-in provider, such as your name and email address.
- **Order and delivery information:** products ordered, quantities, order status, delivery address, and contact details needed to fulfill an order.
- **Uploaded content:** images or files that you voluntarily select and upload through features of the application, where applicable.
- **Notification information:** a device/app notification token may be processed to send push notifications, such as order updates.
- **Technical information:** device type, operating-system information, application information, connectivity information, and diagnostic information where needed for security, troubleshooting, and application functionality.
- **Location information:** only when a location feature is used and the required permission is granted, such as for map or delivery-related functionality.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Authenticate users and secure access to the application.
- Process orders and arrange delivery.
- Provide customer support and communicate about orders.
- Send relevant push notifications, including order and service updates.
- Display maps or location-related features when requested by the user.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the application.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Cozmo Mart may use third-party services to provide application functionality. Depending on the features you use, these may include:

- **Firebase Authentication** for account authentication.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** for push notifications.
- **Google Sign-In** and/or **Sign in with Apple** when you choose those login methods.
- **Google Maps** for map and location-related functionality.
- **Cloudinary** for storing and serving uploaded images where applicable.
- Other hosting, infrastructure, email, analytics, or technical providers used by the application or its backend.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary to provide the service, including with delivery providers, payment providers, technical service providers, or authorities when required by law.

## 5. Payment Information

If online payment is offered, payment details may be processed by the selected payment provider.

We do not intentionally store complete credit or debit card numbers on our own servers unless explicitly stated to the user.

## 6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Data Retention
